What Is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) — And Why Dutch Companies Hire Them Instead of AI Agencies

If you have been following engineering teams at Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Scale AI, you have probably noticed the fastest-growing title in tech right now: the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE).

Now, Dutch scale-ups and enterprises across Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, and Eindhoven are adopting the same model — and for good reason. Traditional AI consulting is broken.

Why Traditional AI Projects Stall in the "Last Mile"

Most companies in the Netherlands don't fail at AI because the models aren't smart enough. They fail in the last mile:

  • Consultancies spend 8 weeks interviewing stakeholders and hand over a 60-page PDF roadmap.
  • Isolated PoC teams build a shiny demo in a Jupyter notebook that cannot connect to your legacy ERP, PostgreSQL database, or authentication layer.
  • Recruiting a full-time senior AI engineer in Amsterdam takes 4 to 7 months — while competitors ship now.

Enter the Forward Deployed Engineer

A Forward Deployed Engineer is a senior, product-minded AI & software engineer who embeds directly inside your company — sitting in your Slack, joining your standups, and working on-site or hybrid with your team.

Instead of separating "business analysts" from "developers", an FDE does both:

  1. Talks directly to your domain experts (operations, finance, legal, or customers) to pinpoint the exact bottleneck.
  2. Reverse-engineers your existing codebase and messy data pipelines.
  3. Ships production AI software inside your private VPC (AWS, Azure, GCP, or on-premise) in days, not quarters.
  4. Hardens & hands over the system with automated tests, LLM evaluations, EU AI Act guardrails, and clean documentation.
