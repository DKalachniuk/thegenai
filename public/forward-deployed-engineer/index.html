Forward Deployed Engineer — FAQ

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)?

A Forward Deployed Engineer (pioneered by Palantir and adopted by OpenAI and Anthropic) is a senior software and AI engineer who embeds directly inside a client's team. Unlike consultants who deliver slide decks, an FDE integrates AI into your real systems and ships production code.

Can I hire a Forward Deployed Engineer on-site in the Netherlands?

Yes. We are based in Amsterdam and work on-site or hybrid across Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, Eindhoven, and The Hague, as well as remote across the EU.

What tech stacks and AI models do you work with?

We work inside your existing stack (Python, TypeScript/Node.js, Go, Java, .NET, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes) and deploy private or frontier models (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, open-weights on Azure/AWS Bedrock/GCP Vertex) inside your VPC.

How do you ensure GDPR and EU AI Act compliance?

All deployments run in your private EU VPC or on-premise with zero data retention and zero training on your proprietary data. We build audit logging, PII redaction, and EU AI Act compliance from day one. See our Security & Data Handling page.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do — 100%. All custom code, pipelines, prompts, and documentation are committed directly to your repositories with zero vendor lock-in.
